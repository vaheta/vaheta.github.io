[LookAtArts](https://lookatarts.com) is a daily visual meditation: one artwork a day from the Met’s [open-access collection](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access), presented with nothing competing for your attention. The project was sparked by a [New York Times story](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2024/07/20/upshot/attention-experiment.html) about Harvard art history professor Jennifer Roberts, who encourages her students to spend three full hours with a single artwork in a museum. In a world where a painting someone spent months on gets half a second of attention as you scroll past it, that kind of slow, deliberate observation felt like a radical idea — and a powerful way to re-center and find calm. Me and my friend [Petr Kaplunovich](https://x.com/petrkaplunovich) wanted to build a small corner of the internet around it.

A new piece appears every day — visit [lookatarts.com](https://lookatarts.com) and take a minute. We hope it helps you carve out a moment of presence in your day.
